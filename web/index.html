NIO ▾

❓ Help & Shortcuts

Nio is your local AI friend. Everything runs 100% on your machine — your chats, memory, and models never leave it.

🌐 Recommended browser: Microsoft Edge (best Text-to-Speech voices), then Google Chrome. Nio opens one of them automatically — Edge first.

Chat

EnterSend the message.
Shift + EnterAdd a new line in the message box.
EscapeClose an open window (Models, Help, popovers).
⏸ PauseStop Nio mid-answer and redirect it. Appears while it is working.

Header buttons

k ctxContext length — how much of the conversation the model can see at once. Click the chip to change it (8k → 32k). A bigger context uses more memory, so keep it low on a light PC. Nio keeps your conversation inside the limit automatically.
🔊 TTSToggle Text-to-Speech — Nio reads its replies aloud.
🎤 LiveContinuous hands-free voice conversation mode.
❓ HelpThis window.
⚙️ SettingsTTS voice and speech-recognition language.
ThemeMarble look.
New chatStart a fresh conversation.
🪟 Window sizeResize the Nio window however you like — Nio remembers it. It re-opens at your size (centered on screen) next time you launch. Your size is saved to your install, so it's private to you.

Message-box buttons

🎤 MicVoice dictation — speak, and your words appear in the box.
SendSend the message (same as Enter).

What Nio can do

FilesRead files — limited to your home folder and workspace. Sensitive paths are blocked.
WebSearch the web and read pages for up-to-date answers.
MemorySay “remember this” and Nio saves the fact to recall it in future sessions.

Uninstall

Just delete the Nio.AppImage and the Nio folder in your Home folder.
